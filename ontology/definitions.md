# Ontology Definitions (v0.1)

## Levels of similarity

- **Level I — Generic metaphysical:** unity, change, hidden reality, interconnectedness, consciousness-as-important. Weak evidence for quantum correspondence.
- **Level II — Classical / field-like:** distributed substrate, local state, propagation, oscillation, mediation, continuous structure. May resemble classical fields/EM — **not automatically quantum**.
- **Level III — Quantum-specific:** discrete allowed states, fundamental probability, nonseparability, contextuality, incompatible observables, measurement-conditioned outcomes, quantized excitations.

## Families

See `ontology_v0.1.yaml`: fundamental_ontology, dynamics, quantum_specific, relational, observer_epistemic, field_like.

## Scoring implications

Missing/NA/U never count as positive evidence. QEF uses only quantum-specific feature IDs listed in `physics_fingerprints/index.yaml`.
