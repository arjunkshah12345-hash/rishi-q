# Worked examples (instrument checks)

## Example A — Generic unity (must NOT yield Q06=1)

> Everything is one. All things are interconnected.

Expected: possible weak O01; **Q06 = 0 or NA** (not 1).

## Example B — Classical EM field (field-like, not quantum)

> A classical electromagnetic field varies across space and mediates interactions; disturbances propagate.

Expected: F01–F05 positive; **F07/Q08 not automatic**.

## Example C — Nonseparability

> The joint state cannot be represented as independent component states.

Expected: **Q06 = 1** with that span as evidence.

## Example D — Statistical ignorance

> Probability only reflects our ignorance of hidden definite values.

Expected: M03 possible; **Q03 ≠ 1**.
