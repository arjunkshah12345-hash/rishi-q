# RISHI-Q Annotation Codebook (ontology v0.1)
Status: **PILOTED** — not frozen for confirmatory use.
## Global rules
Labels: `1` explicitly supported; `0` explicitly contradicted; `NA` insufficient; `U` ambiguous (non-positive for scoring).
**Prefer NA over 1.** Positive labels require an exact evidence span from the passage.
### Hard interpretation rules
- Everything is one does NOT imply entanglement (Q06).
- Interconnected does NOT imply nonlocality.
- Vibration does NOT imply QFT.
- Prana does NOT automatically mean physical energy.
- Akasa does NOT automatically mean a physical/quantum field.
- Sakti does NOT automatically mean physics energy.
- Anu does NOT automatically mean elementary particle.
- Observer importance does NOT imply quantum measurement.
- Epistemic uncertainty does NOT imply quantum indeterminacy.
- Invisible does NOT imply quantum.
- When ambiguous, prefer NA/U over 1.
- Positive labels require exact evidence spans.

## Features

### O01 — Fundamental substrate
- **Family:** fundamental_ontology  
- **Level:** I (not quantum-specific; not field-like)
- **Definition:** An underlying entity or level is presented as ontologically more fundamental than observable objects.
- **Positive requirements:** Explicit claim that some substrate/level is more basic than observed objects
- **Negative conditions:** Mere hierarchy of social status or ritual purity without ontology of matter/reality
- **Exclusions:** Generic 'deeper meaning' of a story without ontological substrate
- **Ambiguity:** If only poetic depth is claimed without an underlying entity, label NA.
- **Positive example:** Observable forms arise from an underlying reality that persists while forms change.
- **Negative example:** The king is more important than the subjects.
- **Ambiguous example:** A poetic or metaphorical phrasing that hints at the idea without an explicit structural claim — label **NA** or **U**, never **1**.
- **Distinction:** Do not confuse with superficially similar Level I metaphors; see exclusions and hard rules.
