# FAQ for skeptical readers (Discussion companion)

## “Isn't this just motivated reasoning for Hindu exceptionalism?”

No. The primary design requirement is that a negative result be publishable and respected. Controls include Buddhist, Jain, Greek, Chinese, mystical, and Sanskrit literary negatives. Decisions that would be chosen only to make Sanskrit look quantum-like are logged and forbidden.

## “Won't embeddings find quantum vibes everywhere?”

Embeddings are secondary. Primary evidence is feature-wise ontology labels with evidence spans. Training contamination is treated as a threat model, not a feature.

## “Everything is one = entanglement?”

Explicitly forbidden (hard rule + tests).

## “Why trust an AI annotator?”

We do not. Human validation is required before confirmatory claims. Local heuristic backends are for instrument tests only.

## “Where are the Sanskrit results?”

Not in this manuscript. Confirmatory analysis is locked until preregistration. Exploratory exhibits use synthetic + modern-physics controls.

## “What would convince you the exciting hypothesis is wrong?”

Negligible ΔQ; classical fields explain the pattern as well as QM/QFT; masking/translation destroy the effect; humans disagree on Level III features; one text or one model drives everything.
