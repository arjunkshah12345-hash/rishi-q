# Ethical statement

RISHI-Q studies historical philosophical texts using computational methods.

- Do not claim that religious scriptures “proved” modern physics.
- Do not sensationalize results for engagement.
- A negative or mixed result is a valid and preferred outcome over a forced positive.
- Respect copyright and cultural context of source traditions.
- Human annotators should be credited; do not invent their judgments.
- Expert Sanskrit and physics review is required before strong public claims.
