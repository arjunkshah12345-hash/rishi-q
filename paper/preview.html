Flagship exploratory finding

We did not find that the Upaniṣads contain quantum mechanics. We found something more scientifically valuable: under heuristic v0.3, Vedānta PD English shows rich Level I substrate/manifestation structure (Brahman/Ātman language) with zero quantum-family positives, while the same pipeline detects classical void/atomism cues in Lucretius and retains Q-family hits on modern physics controls. Separately, the PD Paramananda sample contains modern scientific anachronisms (e.g. electrons) — so some “physics-like” resonance is editorial modernization, not Upaniṣadic Level III structure. ΔQ remains ≈ 0.
